## the logo

the confy C is a tilted rounded square with a horizontal bar and circle cutting through it to form a C shape. the interior is divided into colored panels, each representing a different type of confy user.

### color lore

the panels aren't just decoration. each color means something:

- **green** - new linux users looking for a solution to dotfile hell. green is also a nod to a common starter distro.
- **red** - seasoned sysadmins looking to up their game. red for RHEL.
- **blue** - linux nerds and tinkerers who break things often and need a reliable backup for their configs. the shade might look familiar if you know your distro logos.
- **yellow** - people actively learning linux who want something to help them feel comfortable in the terminal. a nod to ubuntu's earlier days.
- **purple** - the maintainer's favorite color
